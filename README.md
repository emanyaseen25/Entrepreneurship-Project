# UVio – Smart Portable Hygiene Keychain
> **Clean Anywhere, Anytime**
## Overview
UVio is an entrepreneurship project developed to address the growing need for portable and convenient hygiene solutions in a post-COVID world. The product is a compact **3-in-1 hygiene keychain** that combines a **UV-C sterilizer**, **mini sanitizer spray**, and **LED flashlight** into a single portable device.
The project focuses on solving everyday hygiene challenges by providing an affordable, reusable, and eco-friendly solution for disinfecting frequently used personal items such as mobile phones, keys, earphones, masks, and currency notes. 
---
## Problem Statement
Traditional hygiene products such as sanitizer bottles and wipes are often:
- Inconvenient to carry
- Harmful to electronic devices
- Disposable and environmentally unfriendly
- Easy to forget during travel
UVio was designed to provide a practical alternative that promotes everyday cleanliness without these limitations.
